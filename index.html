Data Privacy & Governance Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Kosowsky Consulting is committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy, security, and regulatory compliance. This policy outlines our strict protocols regarding the collection and management of third-party utility and telemetry data.

1. Utility Data Authorization & Consent

We strictly utilize authorization frameworks, including the Green Button Connect My Data standard and secure OAuth 2.0 protocols, to access utility energy data. Kosowsky Consulting never requests, stores, or collects raw customer utility account passwords or login credentials. Access is granted solely through explicit, customer-approved tokens that can be revoked by the user at any time.

2. Data Protection & Security Controls

All authorized interval data, billing records, and metadata are encrypted both in transit (using TLS 1.3) and at rest (using AES-256 encryption standards). Data access is restricted strictly to programmatic analysis tools required to fulfill client-requested optimization services.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing

Kosowsky Consulting operates under a strict non-disclosure framework. We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute client utility data, profile metrics, or business credentials to any third-party marketing entities, data brokers, or external platforms.

4. Data Retention and Erasure

Historical utility usage data is retained only for the duration necessary to complete the specified analytical scope. Clients retain the right to request immediate and permanent erasure of all hosted database records and authorization tokens at any time.
